# Closest-work full-text notes

Read on 2026-10-26. These studies were read, not reproduced.

## Hong (2026), DATS, arXiv:2609.13890v1
- Read main paper, ablations, limitations and reproducibility appendix: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13890v1.
- Predicts success of a fixed catalogue of collaboration topologies from problem features.
- The graph head connects candidate topologies, not our disrupted scheduling constraints.
- Selection precedes execution; verifier-driven escalation is explicitly left open.
- Cached outcomes permit cost-budget calibration inside training folds.
- Backbone transfer needs recalibration; the mathematics check is retrained.
- Overlap is cost-aware collaboration selection. Our contribution is a restricted post-disruption signal measurement, not the invention of routing.
- Direct cross-paper performance comparisons across these tasks are unwarranted.

## Banerjee and Chadha (2026), Routed Graph Handoff
- Read all ten pages, including appendices: https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.2008.pdf; extracted copy in source_cache.
- A prompt-based classifier selects typed graph or natural-language format before each delegation.
- The graph-aware executor prompt matters; serialization alone is insufficient.
- Measures answer quality, tokens and latency under routed and fixed-format conditions.
- Routing is coarse task-type selection; execution-time switching remains future work.
- Headline and appendix token denominators differ, precluding a direct numerical compression comparison here.
- Overlap is adaptive delegation, not post-disruption repair auditing; corrected the draft's once-before-task description.

## Kim and colleagues (2026 revision), arXiv:2512.08296v3
- Read main methods, results, scaling model, validation, discussion and limitations: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v3.
- Compares single-agent and communication architectures over heterogeneous domains.
- Distinguishes parallelizable from sequential tasks and measures coordination overhead.
- Regression uses capability and coordination metrics; configuration and domain holdouts answer different questions.
- Several metrics need execution traces and are not free routing features.
- The revision changes benchmark coverage and estimates; cite v3 without mixing earlier numbers.
- Supports task-dependent coordination benefits, not impossibility of useful structural signals.
- Our check-omission model does not reproduce their real-model experiments or test their scaling equation.
